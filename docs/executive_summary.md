# Executive Summary: Do Late Deliveries Cause Bad Reviews?

## Bottom line
Yes, late deliveries are strongly linked to bad reviews. Late orders are only
7% of all orders, but they bring about a third (32%) of all 1–2 star reviews.

## Key numbers
- 6 in 10 late orders (62%) get a bad review, vs 1 in 10 on-time orders (9%).
- Late orders score 2.3 stars on average, vs 4.3 stars for on-time orders.
- 6,534 of 96,478 delivered orders (6.8%) arrived after the promised date.

## Recommendation
1. Reduce late deliveries first: fixing them could remove up to a third of
   bad reviews.
2. Next analysis: find which sellers and regions have the most late orders,
   so the logistics team can target the biggest problems.

## Note
This shows a strong link, not proof of cause. Other factors (for example,
one seller who both ships late and sends damaged items) could play a part.
